How It Works

Hold Choose Claim

Four steps, no hidden ones. Your wallet decides your options — never your odds.

I

Hold

Your PANTHEA balance unlocks gods. Every holder fields at least one; larger balances unlock all three, and with it more ways to play a round.

II

Choose

Both players pick a god blind, then reveal at the same time. The matchup settles it — a single god beats the wallet that fielded three.

III

Claim

Winners claim from the reserve. Losers keep every token they held — a lost round costs you rank, never your balance.

IV

Reserve

Trading fees feed the reserve that pays winners. Rewards come from trading activity, not from the players who lost.
